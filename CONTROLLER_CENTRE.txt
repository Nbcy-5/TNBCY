CONTROLLER CENTRE (automatic controller)
========================================

What it does while you drive
- Starts the other (AI) train at its timetable time. It never makes you wait more than 15 minutes.
- Makes the AI train stop only at the stations of its timetable ("stops") and run through the others.
- Gives every train a track and platform (main line, loop line, Secunderabad yard track), sets the switches for it
  and keeps a train from using a platform that another train holds.
- Holds a signal at red when no platform is free or the track ahead is occupied, and lets it go afterwards.
- About 2 km before a station (measured to where the station begins, its first switch) a panel offers you the free
  platforms. Occupied or reserved ones are greyed out and say who has them. Tap one and the controller sets the
  switches for it. If you do nothing, it picks one for you about 480 m before the switch.
  If no platform is free the home signal stays red until one is.
  Single-platform stations (Ghatkesar) are allotted without a question.
- Loop lines are real platforms: you can stop there. Your trip ends when you stop at the destination of your train.

Where to look
- Home screen: CONTROLLER CENTRE button. During a drive: pause, then CONTROLLER (top right).
- CONTROL ROOM: autopilot on/off, what the other train is doing, your next platform, the controller log.
- TRAIN SCHEDULES: retime (+/-5 min), delay (+/-5 min), cancel / restore, reset. Saved in user://controller.json.
  Cancelled trains do not appear in SELECT TRAIN and never run as the AI train.
- PLATFORMS, SIGNALS, SWITCHES: live views (read only).

Which train is the AI train
1. The train named by "aiPartner" in the player's entry in Data/trains.json (Deccan Express uses 87625,
   so it follows the Hyderabad Intercity Passenger and the two compete for the Secunderabad platforms).
2. Otherwise the first train that starts on the opposite track (down / up).
3. Otherwise the first other train.
With autopilot off the game picks the other train itself, as before.

Trains in Data/trains.json
The game reads: name, file (consist), desc, kind, coaches, origin, destination, locomotive, platform,
trainNumber, routeStartEdge, routeStartOffsetM, routeSection.
The controller also reads:
  days, distanceKm, departure ("HH:MM"), arrival ("HH:MM"),
  stops: [ { "station": "Moula Ali (MLY)", "arr": "HH:MM", "dep": "HH:MM" } ]  (station names as in the network file; a train with no stops list stops everywhere)
  finalStop: "Charlapalli (CHZ)"   the trip ends when the player stops at this station
  aiPartner: 87625                 the train number to run as the other service
Put the consist in Data/consist_<name>.json and add it to export_files of the "Content" preset.
Platform names shown in the popup come from Data/platforms.json (by track id).

Adding a new module later
1. Write a class that extends ControllerModule (see Scripts/Controller/PlatformModule.cs).
2. Register it in Main.InstallControllerCentre (Scripts/MainController.cs):
   ccCentre.AddModule(new MyModule());
   A new tab appears automatically.
